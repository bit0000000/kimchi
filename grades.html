PSLE Score

The PSLE Score is the sum of four Achievement Levels — English, Mother Tongue, Mathematics, and Science. Range is 4 (best) to 32 (weakest). Lower is better.

Posting Groups

Used for Secondary 1 posting. Determines which subject levels a student can offer in secondary school.

Students in PG1 or PG2 can offer subjects at a higher level if they do well, and PG3 students can offer subjects at a lower level.

Note: Bands and rules are set by MOE and can change. This is a reference summary — always check the official MOE website for the definitive version.
