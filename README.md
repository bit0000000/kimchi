# Grade Tools

Free calculators and reference tools for Singapore students — PSLE and O Level
aggregates, weighted-assessment planning, and school cut-offs.

**Live site:** https://your-username.github.io/grade-tools/

---

## What's inside

| Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Overview and quick-start guide |
| **Score** (`basic.html`) | PSLE Achievement Level conversion · O Level L1R5 / L1R4 |
| **Dream** (`dream.html`) | Enter your own components and weights — see what you need on what's left for every grade |
| **Reverse** (`reverse.html`) | Enter your current grades and a target — see which subject to improve first |
| **Schools** (`schools.html`) | Previous-year intake cut-offs for JCs, Polytechnics, and Secondary schools |

Everything runs in the browser. No accounts, no backend, no tracking.
Your inputs stay on your device.

---

## Design

- **Montserrat** typeface, weights 300–600
- **Six switchable colour palettes** — Pure Monochrome, Warm Minimal, Cool Minimal,
  Scandinavian, Japandi, Desert Minimal
- Light, editorial feel — hairline dividers, no shadows, no gradients
- Fully responsive down to ~375px
- Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Your palette choice persists across pages via `localStorage`
